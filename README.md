# Murder Custom

Murder Custom is a casual fork of [MechanicalMind's Murder](https://github.com/MechanicalMind/murder) for private games with friends. It keeps the original one-murderer, one-gun deception loop, but trades some of its anonymous roleplay for familiar names, extra toys, more clues, and funnier round endings.

One player is secretly the murderer and must eliminate everyone else. The bystanders have to identify and stop them, but only one starts with the gun.

## How this fork differs

Compared with [upstream `master` at `72b9d98`](https://github.com/MechanicalMind/murder/commit/72b9d98), this fork:

- Uses Steam display names instead of randomly generated bystander aliases, making it easier to recognize and call out friends.
- Reworks disguises so the murderer copies a corpse's model and color without spending loot, while keeping their Steam name.
- Adds an in-game picker for cosmetic knife and bystander-weapon variants, including bows and pistols.
- Restricts corpse dragging to the murderer and lets living bystanders inspect bodies for the victim, time and cause of death, and evidence that the body was moved.
- Adds an end-of-round board with up to three highlights for clutch shots, perfect murderer rounds, first deaths, loot collecting, team kills, and speedruns.
- Removes the Melonbomber scoreboard promotion.
- Ships as the separate **Murder Custom** gamemode, with explicit Workshop targeting and Linux packaging and publishing scripts.

## Requirements

Requires **Counter-Strike: Source** to be owned and installed. Missing maps, textures, or models usually mean its content is unavailable.
